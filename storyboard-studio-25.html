🕊️ STRESS-FREE & CALM AUDIO-VISUAL ENFORCER

On rehne par har scene ke prompt mein slow/smooth camera lock lagta hai (whip pan, crash zoom, handheld shake, tez cuts khud naram ho jate hain), negative prompt mein jerky/flicker blockers judte hain, aur har export block mein peaceful audio direction aati hai (setting ke mutabiq: deep-sea hums, soft cosmic drone, room-tone wagaira). Harsh SFX alfaz (explosion, siren, scream, thunder clap) export mein khud naram ho jate hain; aapka saved SFX text wahi rehta hai. Ye Atmosphere preset ki lighting lock ke saath mil kar kaam karta hai — roshni ka source preset hi tay karta hai.

Prompt lamba ho to "⚡ Compact Prompt" ek click mein sirf zaroori Character DNA, Style, Lighting aur core action rakh kar chhota version bana deta hai (original data nahi badalta, kabhi bhi full wapas la sakte hain).

Sirf jin scenes mein wakai masla tha unki action/visual notes/duration adjust hui hai — Character DNA, Atmosphere Lock aur Urdu dialogue/narration bilkul nahi chhue gaye.
